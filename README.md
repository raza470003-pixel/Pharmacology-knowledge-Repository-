## Introduction
An open, structured educational resource that follows drugs from molecular target to clinical use and safety. It is for B.Pharm, Pharm.D and M.Pharm students, GPAT and NIPER aspirants, medical and healthcare students, and researchers.

## Mission
- Explain pharmacology accurately, in original words, with traceable references.
- Link classification, mechanism, uses, adverse effects, interactions and comparisons.
- Stay open to community correction.

## Scope
Drugs covered are limited to the 14 areas listed in [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md).
